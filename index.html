Our story

One oven, one rule:
nothing frozen, ever.

We started with a second-hand bread oven and a stubborn idea that a neighbourhood café can cook like a restaurant. Every morning the fire is lit at 4:30, the first loaves come out at 6, and the menu follows whatever our farmers bring in that week.

  • 🔥

    Real fire

    Oak and olive wood only. You taste it in the crust.

  • 🌾

    Local grain

    Stone-ground flour from two family mills.

  • ☕

    Fresh roast

    Beans roasted less than 10 days before brewing.
